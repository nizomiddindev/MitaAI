## Release 0.5.2 Update (6 October 2026)

- **Subtitles Preserved on Pause & Focus Loss**:
  - Root cause resolved: `OnApplicationFocus(false)` previously invoked `ResetBinding()`, which called `native.Hide()` and erased active subtitle playback and active turns whenever the window lost focus (e.g. Alt-Tab or clicking another window). Additionally, `NativePresentation` previously checked `turnCamera.isActiveAndEnabled` in `bindingLost`, causing turns to cancel if the camera disabled during pause.
  - Fix: Decoupled focus loss and pause from subtitle teardown. When `OnApplicationFocus(false)` occurs, only the composer input panel is stowed (`CloseComposer` + `HideComposer`); active subtitles, queued turns, target bindings, and background AI requests remain completely untouched.
  - Screen subtitles and turn countdowns use `PauseTick(Time.unscaledDeltaTime)` sliding deadlines: while paused (`controller.isPaused`, `!Application.isFocused`, or `Time.timeScale <= 0`), subtitle reveal and expiry deadlines slide forward in lockstep with the frame delta, freezing the text in place on the screen (including on the Pause menu) and resuming cleanly upon unpause.
- **In-Game Chat History Overlay (`H` Key - Subtitle-Style Overhaul)**:
  - User can press `H` at any time outside typing to toggle the Chat History overlay.
  - Can be dismissed via `H`, `Escape`, or clicking the top-right `[×]` close button.
  - **Aesthetic Overhaul (Matching In-Game Subtitle Style)**:
    - Redesigned from a center-screen box into a wide, translucent bottom panel positioned directly in the subtitle band (88% screen width up to 1540px, 28% screen height up to 240px, anchored 34px above screen bottom). Leaves over 72% of the screen completely clear.
    - Deep translucent magenta background (62% opacity) with a soft neon pink outline (40% opacity), 14px rounded corners, and subtle top-rim highlight.
    - Automatically adopts MiSide's authentic game font (`Rounded Mplus 1c`) via `native.NativeFont`.
    - Automatically fades out the active subtitle panel (`native.Tick(..., historyOpen: true)`) while history is open to eliminate visual overlap.
    - Speaker labels styled with rich text: `"You:"` in golden orange (`#ffa626`), `"Mita:"` in companion pink (`#ff6ec7`), dialogue in crisp readable off-white (`#faf6fc`).
    - Integrated vertical scrollbar ("scrolling side thing") on the right margin with interactive mouse drag, smooth mouse wheel scrolling (`EventType.ScrollWheel`), and keyboard navigation (Up/Down/PageUp/PageDown/Home/End).
  - **IL2CPP Stripping Immunity**: Built with pure unstripped primitives (`GUI.Label`, `GUI.Button`, `FillRounded`, custom event math); zero dependency on stripped `GUI.BeginScrollView`. Wrapped in non-fatal try/catch to ensure gameplay interactions and E prompts never fail.
  - **Camera Look & Movement Suppression**:
    - When Chat History is opened (`OpenHistory()`), the mod discovers the active `PlayerMove` controller, saves previous states (`enabled` and `stopMouseMove`), and suspends the controller (`player.enabled = false; player.stopMouseMove = true;`).
    - This stops the player from rotating/looking around and walking while moving the mouse or navigating the chat history overlay.
    - While open, `LateUpdate` maintains controller suppression.
    - When closed via `H`, `Escape`, `[×]`, story dialogue (`StoryBusy`), or scene unload, the player controller and mouse look are cleanly restored (`player.enabled` and `player.stopMouseMove` restored to prior states), and cursor lock is restored.
  - Cursor lifecycle: Unlocks and displays mouse cursor while history is open; restores cursor lock to gameplay upon closing (unless game is paused).
  - Storage: `AIChatClient` retains `SessionHistory` (up to 100 turns) separate from the LLM context prompt history (6 turns). Session history persists across gameplay scene transitions, only resetting when returning to `SceneMenu` or exiting the game.

## Build And Install

Close MiSide before replacing the plugin DLL, then run from the workspace:

```powershell
.\tools\Build-InstallProbe.ps1
```

The installer builds against the local loader/game interop and records a DLL hash in `BepInEx/plugins/MitaAI/install-manifest.json`. It rejects overwriting a DLL that differs from its manifest. Original game binaries were fingerprinted in `reports/environment-2026-10-05.json` and remained unchanged after installation.

For logs, look for `MITAAI_PLUGIN_LOADED`, `MITAAI_MAIN_THREAD_READY`, `MITAAI_MITA`, `MITAAI_CAPTION_SHOWN`, and the `MITAAI_PROBE_FAILED` / `MITAAI_PRESENTATION_FAILED` error markers. Keep game/interop binaries local and out of source distributions.
